# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you accept walk-ins?
The clinic operates primarily on a booked-appointment basis to minimize wait
times for patients. Walk-ins are seen if a slot is available, but booking
ahead is strongly recommended.

## Can I book an appointment for someone else, like a family member?
Yes — when booking, simply provide the patient's own name and phone number
(not the caller's) so the appointment is correctly registered under that
patient's profile.

## What should I bring to my appointment?
Please bring a valid ID and, if applicable, your insurance card. If this is
a follow-up visit, bringing any previous prescriptions or test results is
helpful for the doctor.

## Can I request a specific doctor?
Yes — patients can request any active doctor by name when booking, subject
to that doctor's availability. Patients can also set a preferred doctor on
their profile so future bookings default to that doctor automatically.

## What if I need to see a doctor urgently?
For urgent same-day concerns, call the clinic directly rather than booking
through the standard scheduling flow, as urgent slots are managed separately
from routine bookings. For medical emergencies, please contact emergency
services immediately rather than the clinic.

## Is telehealth available?
Telehealth availability depends on the specific doctor and specialty — ask
during booking whether an in-person or remote consultation is preferred, and
the doctor's availability will reflect what's currently offered.
