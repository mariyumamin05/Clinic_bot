# Clinic Policies



## Appointment Policy

Appointments can be booked up to 60 days in advance. Each standard appointment

is 30 minutes long unless otherwise noted by the doctor. Patients are asked to

arrive 10 minutes before their scheduled time to complete any necessary

paperwork. If a patient arrives more than 15 minutes late, the appointment may

need to be rescheduled to protect the schedules of other patients.



## Cancellation Policy

Appointments can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 4 hours

before the scheduled time. Cancellations made with less than 4 hours' notice,

or missed appointments without any notice ("no-shows"), may be subject to a

missed-appointment fee at the clinic's discretion. Repeated no-shows may

require future appointments to be pre-paid.



## Rescheduling Policy

Patients may reschedule an existing appointment to any other available slot

with the same doctor, subject to normal availability. There is no limit on

the number of times an appointment can be rescheduled, provided each change

is made with reasonable notice per the cancellation policy above.



## Clinic Timings

The clinic is open Monday through Saturday. Each doctor sets their own

specific weekly hours, which may vary - the booking system will always show

real, current availability for the specific doctor requested. The clinic is

closed on public holidays and any other days explicitly marked as closed in

the scheduling system.



## Payment & Insurance

The clinic accepts cash and major debit/credit cards at time of visit. The

clinic works with a range of local insurance providers for partial or full

coverage of consultation fees - patients should confirm their specific

provider's coverage with clinic staff, as coverage varies by plan. A payment

receipt is provided after every visit for insurance reimbursement purposes.
