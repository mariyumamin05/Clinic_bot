# Services \& Doctors



## Available Specialties

The clinic currently offers the following specialties: Cardiology,

Dermatology, and General Physician consultations. The exact list of active

doctors and their specialties is always available live through the booking

system - this document gives general background, not a live roster.



## Cardiology

Our cardiology service covers consultations for heart health concerns,

including routine check-ups, blood pressure management, and referrals for

further cardiac testing when needed. Patients with chest pain, palpitations,

shortness of breath, or a family history of heart disease are encouraged to

book a cardiology consultation.



## Dermatology

Our dermatology service covers skin, hair, and nail concerns - including

acne, rashes, eczema, mole checks, and general skin health consultations.



## General Physician

General physician appointments cover everyday health concerns, general

check-ups, minor illnesses and injuries, and referrals to a specialist when

a condition requires more focused care.



## First-Time Patients

New patients will be asked for their full name and phone number when booking

their first appointment, which creates a patient profile in the system. This

profile is used for all future bookings, so returning patients don't need to

re-register.
